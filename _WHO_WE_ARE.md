# PurTera IT, in facts

Context for the LLM stages and the brief. NOT labels -- see
`_LABELING_DOCTRINE.md` -> "Who we are is context, never a label".

Facts only. No adjectives, nothing aspirational, nothing that reads as
marketing: a model repeats what it is given.

* PurTera IT executes multi-site IT deployments: programmes of roughly 50 to
  500 sites, 350+ locations managed.
* We are the FIELD EXECUTION. We dispatch technicians who mount, cable,
  certify, photograph and close out. On most deals we are the installer.
* Work we do not staff in house is SUBCONTRACTED, not declined. Access control
  on 010288 went to an outside contact rather than being turned away.
* Verticals: retail and hospitality, healthcare and education, corporate
  branches, data centres, telecom, private-equity portfolio operations.
* Typical work: access points, switching, POS refreshes, cabling and
  certification, rack and stack, cutovers.
* Quotes turn around in 24 to 48 hours. Parsing feeds that quote.
* PurPulse is our own platform: site readiness, crew runbooks, proof-of-work
  capture, closeout. The atom labeler lives inside it.
* Our domain is purtera-it.com. An address at that domain is ours, and the
  parser already uses this to tell "ours" from "theirs".
* A reseller (CDW among others) brings us the deal. The end customer owns the
  site. A vendor (Huzzard among others) makes the hardware.
* "Provided by us" IN A CUSTOMER'S OR RESELLER'S DOCUMENT names THEIR
  organisation, not ours.

## What this file must never be used for

Deciding a label. Whether we are the installer on a given deal, who supplies a
given item, and whether we can do a given kind of work are facts about THAT
deal, evidenced in its own documents. This file says what is possible. It never
says what happened.
